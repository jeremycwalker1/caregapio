What you need before your first order

Most teams find at least one of these late. Read them now, not after you have built the checkout.

01

An ordering provider with an NPI

Every lab order needs a licensed clinician behind it. If you have your own physician or a telehealth group, you are ready. If you do not, you need one before you can order — this is not direct-access testing, and CareGap never originates the order.

02

Cash pay, billed to your account

Quest bills your account for the test, and you collect from the patient inside your own app at one price, known at checkout. Insurance billing is not supported today.

03

Your states of practice

You declare where you operate at signup. Some states restrict re-billing lab work through direct-bill, anti-markup, and disclosure rules. We flag those states for you so your pricing decision is an informed one.

04

Somewhere for the patient to live

Real lab results are protected health information the moment they land. Members in a web app are not the same thing as patients on HIPAA rails. That layer is included here, so this one is already handled.

Questions people actually ask

Do I really need an NPI?

Yes. A valid lab order requires a licensed ordering provider, and that is true whether the patient pays cash or not. If you have your own clinician or a telehealth partner, you are covered. If you do not have one yet, tell us in the form below and we will point you at options.

What does a test actually cost me?

The test is billed by Quest to your account, separately from CareGap's platform fee. You order at CareGap account rates rather than Quest published list price. Tell us which panels you care about in the form and we will send your specific rates.

Can I charge my patients more than I pay?

You set your own retail price inside your app. Be aware that roughly half the states regulate re-billing lab work through direct-bill, anti-markup, or disclosure statutes. You declare your states of practice at signup, we flag the ones with restrictions, and your counsel should review before you publish pricing.

Can I bill insurance?

Not today. This is cash pay with the test billed to your account. Insurance billing brings payer enrollment, claims, denials, and appeals into scope, and it is deliberately out of scope for this offering.

Why is the fee flat instead of a percentage?

Because it has to be. EKRA reaches commercial and cash-pay testing, and payments that move with the volume or value of tests are exactly the pattern enforcers look at. A flat fee charged to you, priced to the technology, keeps everyone clean.

Do you support at-home collection?

Yes, as an add-on through our mobile phlebotomy partner, in addition to Quest patient service centers. Flag it in the form and we will include it in your setup.

Is there a BAA?

Yes, and it is part of the click-through package at signup, along with the service agreement and cash-pay attestation. CLIA certification and licensure sit with Quest. Card data is handled by Stripe and never stored by CareGap.

How fast can I be in sandbox?

Sandbox access does not wait on Quest paperwork. You can be calling the API and building against real test data while your account is being issued.
