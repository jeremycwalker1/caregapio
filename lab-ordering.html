What you need to go live

None of this is needed for sandbox. All of it is needed before your first real order.

01

A legal entity

Accounts are for companies, practices and programs, not individuals. We verify the entity before it can order.

02

A licensed ordering provider

Every order is authorized by a clinician with an NPI and a license in the patient's state. Each one verifies their identity and signs with CareGap personally — CareGap never originates an order.

03

Cash pay only

Orders are client-billed to CareGap and charged to you. No insurance, no patient billing, and no federal health care program beneficiaries.

04

A 24/7 critical contact

For each provider, someone who can be reached day or night if a result is critical: the provider, a covering clinician, or an answering service.

Questions people actually ask

Do I need my own Quest account?

No. You order on CareGap's account; Quest bills us and we charge you. If you would rather Quest bill you directly, set up your own account through us instead.

What does a test cost me?

Each test is charged at CareGap's Testing Price List, the same for every customer, plus the per-order platform fee. Ask us for the prices of the panels you need.

Can I try it without a clinician?

In sandbox, yes. Every sandbox account comes with a test ordering provider, so you can place orders end to end before anyone enrolls. Real orders need your own enrolled provider.

What happens if a result is critical?

CareGap relays it to the provider's 24/7 critical contact and records every attempt. We relay only — interpreting the result and following up with the patient stay with your clinician.

Can I charge my patients more than I pay?

You set your own retail price. Many states regulate re-billing lab work through direct-bill, anti-markup or disclosure rules, so have your counsel review your pricing for the states you operate in.

Is there a BAA?

Yes. You declare your HIPAA status at signup, and the BAA you accept applies the right terms to it, whether or not you are a covered entity. Card data is handled by Stripe and never stored by CareGap.

I already have a Quest subaccount through CareGap. Can I switch?

Yes. Email us and we will move your account; you accept the agreements for this model and keep your API keys.
